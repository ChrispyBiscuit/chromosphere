Chromosphere | ULTRA installs:

  VST3 plug-in   ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ChromosphereUltra.vst3
                     The folder FL Studio and every other VST3 host scans.

  Standalone app ->  C:\Program Files\Chromosphere ULTRA\ChromosphereUltra.exe
                     Plays without a DAW. Start menu shortcut, optional desktop shortcut.

Close your DAW and the ULTRA app before you continue. An earlier copy is replaced; your own presets are kept.
