Chromosphere | ULTRA is installed.

STANDALONE APP
  Start menu > Chromosphere | ULTRA. Click "Options" (top left) > Audio/MIDI Settings to choose your audio
  device and turn on your MIDI controller. Press Play to run the sequencer; the pads play the drums too.

FL STUDIO (20.8 or newer)
  1. Options > Manage plugins > "Find plugins" (tick "Verify plugins" the first time).
  2. Type  ULTRA  in the filter box; tick the star in the F column to add it to your favourites.
  3. Add it to the Channel rack: it is an instrument (generator), "Chromosphere | ULTRA".

OTHER DAWS
  Rescan VST3 plug-ins; ULTRA appears as an instrument (drum machine) from "Chromosphere". The sequencer
  follows the host transport. Eight extra stereo outputs (Aux 1-8) can be enabled for multi-out routing.

UPDATES
  ULTRA checks once a day for a new version (one small file from the release page). When one is out, the
  Updates button in the header lights up: click it, then "Install now". Turn on automatic install there to
  have new versions install silently when you close your DAW or the app. Both can be switched off.

PRESETS
  Your own presets are saved in  %APPDATA%\ChromosphereUltra\Presets.

UNINSTALL
  Windows Settings > Apps > Chromosphere | ULTRA > Uninstall.

Windows may warn ("Windows protected your PC") because the installer is not code-signed:
More info > Run anyway.
